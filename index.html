About Sarah

Sarah writes children's picture books for ages 3–6 from her home in Honiton, Devon. With a BA in English Literature and years of storytelling behind her, she creates warm, playful tales rooted in the Devon countryside she loves.

Every book is self-published, hands-on from first draft to final page, and illustrated by the wonderfully talented Emma from nearby Honiton. The stories are read everywhere — from bedtime to story-time at village fetes and primary schools across East Devon.

Wren & Whistle Books is Sarah's own little publishing house, named for two things she loves: the wrens that hop through her garden, and the whistle of the kettle after a long day writing.

— Sarah
