About

Sarah writes children’s picture books for ages 3–6 from her home in Honiton, Devon. With a BA in English Literature and four years of storytelling behind her, she creates warm, playful tales that feel right at home in a small village — because they are.

Every book is self-published, hands-on from first draft to final page, and illustrated by the wonderfully talented Emma from nearby Honiton.

The books

  • The Hedgehog Who Couldn’t Hibernate

    A hedgehog stays awake all winter and discovers a world her sleeping friends have never seen.

  • Barnaby’s Kite

    A boy and his kite soar over the Devon hills in a story about letting go and looking up.

  • The Mud Pie Competition

    A village fete contest goes gloriously wrong when mud pies, judging, and a very wet dog collide.
